## Step 1: Connecting Data to Power BI

1. Open **Power BI Desktop**.
2. Click on **Get Data** on the Home ribbon.
3. Select **Text/CSV** and click **Connect**.
4. Browse to your project folder and select:
   ```
   data/cleaned_sales_data.csv
   ```
5. In the preview window, ensure:
   * **File Origin**: `65001: Unicode (UTF-8)`
   * **Delimiter**: `Comma`
6. Click **Transform Data** (this opens the Power Query Editor).

## Step 3: DAX Measures (Copy & Paste)

In the **Modeling** or **Home** ribbon, click **New Measure** and create the following beginner-friendly DAX measures:

### 1. Total Sales (Revenue)
```dax
Total Sales = SUM(cleaned_sales_data[Sales])
```
*Format: Currency ($), 2 decimal places.*

### 2. Total Profit
```dax
Total Profit = SUM(cleaned_sales_data[Profit])
```
*Format: Currency ($), 2 decimal places.*

### 3. Total Quantity Sold
```dax
Total Quantity = SUM(cleaned_sales_data[Quantity])
```
*Format: Whole Number with thousand separator (,).*

### 4. Overall Profit Margin
```dax
Profit Margin = 
DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
```
*Format: Percentage (%), 2 decimal places.*  
*(Note: We use `DIVIDE` rather than `/` to safely handle division by zero without causing errors).*

### 5. Average Discount Rate
```dax
Average Discount = AVERAGE(cleaned_sales_data[Discount])
```
*Format: Percentage (%), 1 decimal place.*

### 6. Average Order Value (AOV)
```dax
Average Order Value = 
DIVIDE([Total Sales], DISTINCTCOUNT(cleaned_sales_data[Order ID]), 0)
```
*Format: Currency ($), 2 decimal places.*

### 7. Total Orders Count
```dax
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(cleaned_sales_data[Order ID])
```
*Format: Whole Number.*

## Step 4: Building the Visualizations

### Visual 1: Executive KPI Cards (Banner at Top)
* **Visual Type**: `Card` (or `New Card`)
* Create 5 separate cards arranged horizontally across the top:
  1. Card 1: `[Total Sales]` (e.g., **$1.08M**)
  2. Card 2: `[Total Profit]` (e.g., **$146.2K**)
  3. Card 3: `[Profit Margin]` (e.g., **13.56%**)
  4. Card 4: `[Total Quantity]` (e.g., **3,650**)
  5. Card 5: `[Average Discount]` (e.g., **10.0%**)

### Visual 2: Monthly Sales Trend (Time Series)
* **Visual Type**: `Line and Clustered Column Chart` OR `Line Chart`
* **X-Axis**: `Year-Month` (or `Order Date` hierarchy: Year, Month)
* **Y-Axis**: `[Total Sales]`
* **Secondary Y-Axis**: `[Total Profit]`
* **Purpose**: Identifies seasonal peaks, Q4 revenue spikes, and profit correlation over time.

### Visual 3: Sales & Profit by Region
* **Visual Type**: `Clustered Bar Chart` / `Clustered Column Chart`
* **X-Axis**: `Region`
* **Y-Axis**: `[Total Sales]` and `[Total Profit]`
* **Data Labels**: On
* **Purpose**: Reveals that the **East** region leads revenue, followed closely by **North**.

### Visual 4: Sales Share by Category
* **Visual Type**: `Donut Chart`
* **Legend**: `Category`
* **Values**: `[Total Sales]`
* **Detail Labels**: Category + Percent of Total
* **Purpose**: Demonstrates that **Technology** represents 70% of gross revenue, with **Furniture** at 27% and **Office Supplies** at 3%.

### Visual 5: Top 10 Products by Revenue
* **Visual Type**: `Clustered Bar Chart` (Horizontal)
* **Y-Axis**: `Product`
* **X-Axis**: `[Total Sales]`
* **Filters on this visual**:
  * Filter Type: `Top N`
  * Show items: `Top 10`
  * By value: `[Total Sales]`
* **Purpose**: Highlights enterprise laptops (Dell XPS 15, MacBook Air M2, ThinkPad Carbon X1) as high-value revenue drivers.

## Step 5: Interactive Slicers & Filters

Add these 3 interactive slicers on the left sidebar or top filter bar:

1. **Date Range Slicer**:
   * Field: `Order Date`
   * Style: **Between** (Slider)
2. **Region Slicer**:
   * Field: `Region`
   * Style: **Tile** or **Dropdown** (Options: East, North, South, West)
3. **Category Slicer**:
   * Field: `Category`
   * Style: **Dropdown** (Options: Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies)

## Step 6: Dashboard Design & Theme Palette

A clean, modern design ensures clarity and usability for business stakeholders:

* **Canvas Ratio**: 16:9 widescreen (1280 x 720 px)
* **Background Color**: Clean light gray (`#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9`)
* **Card & Visual Containers**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with rounded corners (8px radius) and subtle shadow.
* **Palette Colors**:
  * Primary Accent (Sales): Royal Blue `#2563EB`
  * Success Accent (Profit): Emerald Green `#10B981`
  * Secondary Accent (Furniture): Amber `#F59E0B`
  * Tertiary Accent (Tech): Indigo `#6366F1`
  * Dark Font: Slate Gray `#0F172A`

## 💡 Key Design & DAX Best Practices

1. **Model Simplicity**: A clean flat schema suitable for tabular analytics, converting data types strictly in Power Query, and removing dirty raw strings upfront.
2. **DAX Safety**: Using `DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)` instead of the division slash `/` prevents `#DIV/0!` errors when filtering zero-sales periods.
3. **Actionable Insights**: The dashboard directly informs business teams where margin dilution is happening (e.g., high discounts eroding margins down to 5.8%).
